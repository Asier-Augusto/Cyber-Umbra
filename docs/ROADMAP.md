# Roadmap

> Beta preview. Direction, not commitments or dates. The production work is ahead of what
> this public preview reflects.

## Now — foundations (web)

- Perfect, provenance-tracked **ingestion** of public security knowledge.
- Turn that knowledge into **recoverable value**: high-quality retrieval over the store
  and graph.
- Real **reasoning** over the graph: candidate findings a human finds worth their time.

## Next — governed assessment (web)

- Observation of an authorized web surface through standard tooling, with evidence.
- Tiered, human-authorized active validation, bounded and confined to scope.
- Assessment reporting shaped for a professional engagement.

## Later — beyond web

- Additional domains (e.g. identity/directory, cloud, mobile), each entered only after the
  web pipeline is solid and under the same governance model.

## Non-goals

- Fully autonomous exploitation.
- Shipping an exploit arsenal.
- Anything that operates outside an authorized, scoped engagement.
